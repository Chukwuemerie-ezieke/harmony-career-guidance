# PathVerge Course and University Catalogue

## Scope and Purpose
This document outlines the sources and update policies for the `COURSE_CATALOGUE` integrated into PathVerge. The catalogue provides current, verified, student-friendly course pathways and appropriate university options, emphasizing modern and emerging fields.

## Reverification Policy
All entries in the `COURSE_CATALOGUE` must be reverified at least annually and before each admissions cycle to ensure the accuracy of university programmes and official links.
