# Alternative Pathways Sources

## Purpose and Limitations
PathVerge provides alternative education pathways to help students understand that a university degree is not the only valid route to career success. This feature is intended for exploration and public information only. 
- PathVerge does not process school records or collect personal data for this feature.
- PathVerge does not guarantee admission, credential recognition, funding, or employment outcomes for any of the listed pathways or providers.
- Specific tuition fees, cut-off marks, and accreditation statuses are not shown, as these can change rapidly.

## Pathway Distinctions
- **Polytechnic (ND/HND):** Practical, hands-on diploma programs (National Diploma and Higher National Diploma) suitable for technical and commercial fields.
- **College of Education (NCE):** Nigeria Certificate in Education programs specifically designed to train teachers for basic and junior secondary levels.
- **TVET:** Technical and Vocational Education and Training offering highly specialized, hands-on skills such as electrical installation, welding, and culinary arts.
- **Professional Certification / Digital Skills:** Non-degree learning paths offered by vendors (e.g., AWS, CompTIA) or bootcamps that focus strictly on employability skills.

## Reverification Policy
All alternative pathways and their verified providers must be reverified before every major admission cycle (typically annually) to ensure that the official URLs and program availability are still accurate.

## Important Disclaimer
**Programme availability, entry requirements, accreditation, and progression options can change. Confirm details with the provider, relevant regulator, and official admissions information before applying.**
