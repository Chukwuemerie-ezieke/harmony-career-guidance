# PathVerge Admission Requirements Sources

## Scope and Purpose
This document outlines the sources and update policies for the `ADMISSION_REQUIREMENTS` data layer in PathVerge. The requirements cover general JAMB/O'Level eligibility and readiness for prioritized pathways.

## Limitations
- Requirements represent general guidance for Nigerian university admissions and do not account for every institution's specific criteria or waivers.
- Meeting these requirements does not guarantee admission.
- Specific university cut-off marks, direct-entry criteria, and Post-UTME scoring logic are explicitly excluded to maintain clarity and accuracy.
- Users must always consult the official JAMB brochure/IBASS and their chosen university's admissions portal.

## Reverification Policy
All entries in the `ADMISSION_REQUIREMENTS` should be reverified at least annually and before each admissions cycle.

## Source

All Nigerian admission requirements are sourced generally from the [JAMB IBASS (Integrated Brochure and Syllabus System)](https://jamb.gov.ng/ibass).
